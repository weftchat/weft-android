What is a relay?

A relay is a small server that holds your encrypted messages for a short time, until the person you wrote to picks them up. It works like a sealed, anonymous mailbox:

  • It cannot read anything. Messages are encrypted on your phone before they leave it.
  • It has no accounts and does not know who you are or who you talk to.
  • Messages travel through two relays, so no single server sees both where a message comes from and where it goes.
  • People on different relays talk to each other normally. Each person simply receives on their own relay.

Three ways to use relays

1. Weft relays. Free, and the easiest way to start. Run by the Weft project in the EU.

2. Your own relay. Free software you run on any server you rent (from about €6 a month). You control it completely. The SimpleX network self-hosting guide takes about 30 minutes if you are comfortable with a Linux server.

3. A private managed relay. We set up and maintain a relay used only by you or your organisation. You get the control of your own server without the maintenance.

How it works

  1. Tap Request a relay and tell us the plan and the country you want.
  2. We send you a payment link, by card or crypto.
  3. Within 24 hours your relay is ready. We send you its private address, encrypted.
  4. In Weft, go to Security → Relays → Add your own relay and paste it. Done.
  5. Share the address only with the people who should use it. Anyone without it cannot use your relay.

Good to know

  • Payments happen only on this website, never inside the app, and are never linked to your identity in Weft.
  • Your relay keeps no logs of who connects, and deletes messages once they are delivered or after 21 days.
  • If you stop paying, the relay is shut down and wiped. Nothing is kept.
  • You can move to your own server at any time: just add a new relay in the app.
