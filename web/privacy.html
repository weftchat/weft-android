Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The short version

  • Weft has no accounts. We never ask for your name, phone number or email to use the app.
  • Your messages are end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read them, and our relays do not know who is talking to whom.
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.
  • If you email us to join the beta, we use your address only to send your invite, and delete it when you ask.

1. Who we are

Weft is an open-source messenger (AGPLv3) compatible with the SimpleX network. In this policy, “Weft”, “we” and “us” mean the team that runs the Weft project and this website. You can contact us at weftchat@proton.me.

2. This website

weftchat.com is a static website. It does not set cookies, does not load fonts, scripts or images from third parties, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

The site is hosted by Porkbun. Like any web server, their servers technically receive your IP address and browser details in order to deliver the page, and may keep them briefly in server logs for security. We do not use these logs to track or profile anyone.

3. Joining the beta

The “Join the beta” button opens your own email app with a message to weftchat@proton.me. Nothing is sent until you press send. If you send it, we receive your email address and anything you write, in an encrypted Proton Mail inbox.

  • What we use it for: only to send you your beta invitation and essential beta updates.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • How long we keep it: until the beta ends or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.
  • We never sell, share or publish it. You can use any address you like, including an anonymous one.

4. The Weft app

Weft is designed so that there is as little as possible to collect.

  • No identity. Your identity is a set of keys created on your phone. There is no user ID, username, phone number or email.
  • Messages. Messages, files and calls are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they leave it. Only the people you talk to can read them.
  • Relays. Messages travel through relay servers, which only hold encrypted data in anonymous queues for a limited time and cannot see who sends or receives it. Messages are routed through two relays so that no single server sees both your network address and the destination. You can run and use your own relays.
  • On your phone. Your chats and contacts are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database protected by your PIN. We have no copy and no way to recover them.
  • No tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising or Google services.
  • Permissions. The app asks only for the camera (QR codes and photos), microphone (calls) and notifications. Location data is removed from photos before they are sent.

5. Donations

Donations are optional and happen only on this website, never inside the app. When donations open, card payments will be handled by third-party payment providers under their own privacy policies; we receive only what they share with us to confirm the payment. Donating is never linked to your use of the app.

6. Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Write to weftchat@proton.me and we will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Weft is not intended for children under 16.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced on this website.
