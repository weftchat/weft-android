## Description (Liberapay "statement", English)
I'm building **Weft**, a private messenger for people whose words could cost them: journalists, their sources, politicians, activists and anyone who needs to talk without leaving a trace.

Most "secure" messengers still ask for your phone number. Weft doesn't. There are no accounts, no user IDs and no contact lists stored on any server. It is built on the open SimpleX protocol, so nobody — not even us — can see who talks to whom.

**What Weft does**
- End-to-end encryption, including metadata protection
- No phone number, email or identity to register
- Private relays and optional Tor/.onion routing
- Duress PIN that opens a harmless decoy profile, and emergency wipe
- Disappearing messages, no screenshots, GPS stripped from photos
- Zero Google: built for GrapheneOS and Android, no tracking of any kind

**Why donations**
Weft is free and open source (AGPLv3), with no ads, no investors and no data to sell. Donations pay for relay servers, independent security audits and development time. Donating never unlocks features and is never linked to anyone's chat identity — everyone gets the same privacy.

Thank you for helping people speak freely.
